Shipping

GlowSkin currently delivers skincare products to serviceable locations within India.

  • Shipping is free for orders above ₹999.
  • Orders below ₹999 may have a standard shipping charge of ₹49.
  • Shipping charges, if applicable, are shown during checkout.
  • Delivery availability may depend on the destination pincode.
📦

Delivery

Orders are generally estimated to arrive within 3–7 business days.

  • Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary depending on your location.
  • Delays may occur during holidays, extreme weather or courier-related issues.
  • Please make sure your address and mobile number are correct before placing an order.
  • Once an order has been shipped, tracking information may become available.

Order Cancellation

If you need to cancel an order, contact GlowSkin support as soon as possible.

  • Cancellation requests should be submitted before the order is processed or shipped.
  • Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.
  • Please provide your order ID when contacting support.
Contact Support →
↩️

Returns

We want you to have a smooth shopping experience. If you receive an eligible product that you need to return, contact our support team.

  • Contact support with your order ID and reason for the return.
  • Products may need to meet applicable return conditions before a return is accepted.
  • Some products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene and safety considerations.
  • Do not send a product back without first contacting customer support.
💰

Refunds

If a refund is approved, the applicable refund amount and process will be communicated by our support team.

  • Refund eligibility depends on the reason for the return or cancellation.
  • Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment method.
  • For prepaid orders, refunds may be processed through the applicable payment method.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, additional information may be required to process a refund.
⚠️

Damaged or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or contains an incorrect item, please contact us as soon as possible.

  • Keep the product and original packaging safely.
  • Keep your order ID ready.
  • Photos or other information may be requested to help us investigate the issue.
  • Our support team will guide you through the next steps.

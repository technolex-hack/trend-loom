01

Introduction

Welcome to GlowSkin. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly when you visit or use our website.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected through the GlowSkin website and how that information may be used.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GlowSkin, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information provided during account registration.
  • Email address and mobile number.
  • Delivery address and pincode when placing an order.
  • Order details, products purchased and order status.
  • Messages or information submitted through our contact forms.
  • Basic website usage information required for website functionality.
03

How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Processing and managing customer orders.
  • Providing delivery and order support.
  • Responding to customer enquiries.
  • Maintaining customer accounts.
  • Improving website functionality and customer experience.
  • Communicating important information related to orders or services.
04

Orders & Payments

When you place an order, information required to process and deliver that order may be stored or processed by the website and applicable service providers.

Payment information may be processed through the relevant payment service when a real payment gateway is connected.

Important: The current GlowSkin frontend demo uses browser-based storage for demonstration. It is not a production-grade payment or authentication system.
05

Cookies & Local Storage

GlowSkin may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage or session storage to provide website functionality.

In the current frontend version, browser storage may be used for features such as:

  • Shopping cart information.
  • Wishlist information.
  • Demo customer account information.
  • Demo order information.
  • Temporary checkout and order-session data.
06

Information Sharing

GlowSkin does not intend to sell customer information as part of the basic website experience.

When external services are integrated in the future, information may need to be shared with service providers required to operate those services, such as:

  • Payment service providers.
  • Shipping and delivery partners.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Customer support or communication services.
07

Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website.

However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Production deployments should use appropriate server-side security, encrypted connections, secure authentication and protected databases.

08

Your Choices

Depending on the services available to you, you may have choices regarding your information.

  • You can choose whether to create an account.
  • You can contact us regarding information associated with your enquiries or orders.
  • You can manage certain browser storage through your browser settings.
  • You can contact GlowSkin for privacy-related questions or requests.
09

Children's Privacy

GlowSkin is intended for general shopping purposes. We do not knowingly request personal information from children where such collection is not appropriate.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10

Changes to This Policy

GlowSkin may update this Privacy Policy when website features, services or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact the GlowSkin support team.

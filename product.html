Product Details

GlowSkin Vitamin C Brightening Serum is designed for everyday skincare. Its lightweight texture absorbs quickly and helps improve the appearance of dull and uneven-looking skin.

Use it as part of your daily skincare routine for skin that looks brighter, smoother and more refreshed.

Ingredients

  • Vitamin C
  • Niacinamide
  • Hyaluronic Acid
  • Aloe Vera
  • Glycerin

Benefits

  • ✨ Helps brighten dull-looking skin
  • 🌿 Helps improve uneven skin tone
  • 💧 Provides lightweight hydration
  • 🌟 Helps improve the appearance of dark spots
  • ❤️ Suitable for daily skincare

How to Use

  1. Cleanse your face with a gentle face wash.
  2. Take 2–3 drops of serum.
  3. Gently apply over face and neck.
  4. Follow with moisturizer.
  5. Use sunscreen during the daytime.
